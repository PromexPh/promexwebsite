Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Promex, Inc. ("Promex", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, protects and keeps personal information when you use this website, apply for a job, refer a candidate, or contact us. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission. Where we process information in connection with employment in the United Arab Emirates, we also follow applicable UAE law, including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data.

1. Who we are

Promex, Inc. is a Philippine recruitment agency licensed by the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) to recruit, process and deploy landbased workers (Licence No. DMW-451-LB-09242024-R). We are the personal information controller for the information described in this policy.

Office: 2nd Floor, Vision Building, 163 Pasig Boulevard, Brgy. Bagong Ilog, Pasig City, Philippines. WhatsApp: +63 917 318 9396.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us on WhatsApp

  • Identity details: full name, date of birth, nationality.
  • Contact details: mobile and WhatsApp numbers, city and country of residence, preferred contact method and language.
  • Work profile: experience, skills, languages spoken, employment status, availability, and whether you are in the UAE and your visa type.
  • Files you choose to send us: such as your CV or photos.
  • Referral details: the name, phone number and location of a person you refer, and your own name and contact number.

Information collected later in the process

If you continue to the selection and deployment stages, we will ask for documents required by law or by the employer, such as your passport, birth certificate, police or NBI clearance, medical examination results and contract documents. We request these only when they are needed, through official Promex channels, and we tell you why we need each one. Medical results are sensitive personal information, and we process them only as required for lawful deployment.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the website we may collect technical information such as your device type, browser, approximate location from your IP address, pages viewed and which buttons you click. We use this, with your consent where required, to keep the website secure and to understand how it is used. We record that a WhatsApp button was clicked, but never the content of your messages.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decisions that significantly affect you without human review.

4. Who we share it with

  • Prospective employers in the UAE, and our licensed foreign principal or partner agency there, only after we tell you and you agree.
  • Government agencies such as the DMW, the Overseas Workers Welfare Administration, the Bureau of Immigration, the Philippine Embassy or Consulate, and UAE authorities including the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, where required for lawful deployment.
  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as accredited medical clinics, training centres, travel agents, insurers, IT, hosting and messaging providers. They may use your information only on our instructions and must protect it.
  • Professional advisers and authorities where required by law, court order, or to protect your safety or legal rights.

The person who referred you never receives your application details or progress.

5. International transfers

Because this job is in the UAE, we transfer your information to the UAE for employment purposes. Some of our service providers may also store information outside the Philippines. When we transfer information abroad, we use contractual and other safeguards so it receives a level of protection comparable to Philippine law.

7. How we protect your information

We use organisational, physical and technical safeguards, including encrypted connections, access limited to authorised staff on a need-to-know basis, staff confidentiality obligations, secure storage for documents, and regular review of our security practices. If a personal data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your information is processed;
  • access your information and get a copy of it;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • object to processing, or withdraw your consent, at any time;
  • ask us to erase or block your information where the law allows;
  • receive your information in a portable electronic format;
  • claim damages if you suffer harm from unlawful processing; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp at +63 917 318 9396 starting with "Data Privacy Request". We will verify your identity and respond within 30 days. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already done, but it may mean we can no longer progress your application.

9. Cookies

We use cookies that are needed for the website to work. We also use advertising and analytics tags from Google (including Google Ads) and Meta to measure how many visitors contact us after seeing our ads. These tags record that a WhatsApp button was clicked and which one, but never the content of your messages. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings, and you can manage ad personalisation in your Google and Meta account settings.

10. Age requirement

This job is open to applicants aged 24 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

11. Stay safe from recruitment scams

Promex charges zero placement fee for this job. We will never ask you to pay a fee, or to send your passport or ID, through social media, personal accounts or unofficial numbers. Our official recruitment WhatsApp is +63 917 318 9396. Report anything suspicious to us and to the DMW.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date, and tell you directly about significant changes that affect an application you have made.
